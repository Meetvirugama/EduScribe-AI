## Converting Generative AI to Agentic AI

To understand the conversion of generative AI into **Agentic AI**, it's essential to first grasp the concepts of **Generative AI** and **LLM models**. **Generative AI** refers to a type of AI that involves the use of models to generate content. **LLM models**, or Large Language Models, are a crucial component of generative AI, used for content generation. In the context of **LLM models**, the main task is to query the model and generate content based on a specific prompt. For instance, a prompt might instruct the **LLM model** to act like an author.

### Example
#### Problem
How to query **LLM models** to generate content?
#### Solution
The **LLM model** generates content based on the query and prompt. For example, if the prompt is to act like an author, the **LLM model** will generate content in the style of an author.

The emergence of **Agentic AI** is being discussed in the context of converting generative AI into an agentic application. **Agentic AI** involves the use of **AI agents** or multi-agents to create custom applications. **AI agents** are autonomous entities that can perform tasks, and **Multi-Agent AI** involves the use of multiple **AI agents**. The relationship between **Agentic AI** and **Generative AI** is that **Agentic AI** is derived from **Generative AI**.

### Key Takeaways
* **Agentic AI** is being discussed in the context of converting generative AI into an agentic application.
* **LLM models**, such as Open LLM models or LLaMA, are being used for agentic AI applications.
* The main task of **LLM models** is to query and generate content based on a specific prompt.
* A specific prompt is required to define how the **LLM model** should behave.
